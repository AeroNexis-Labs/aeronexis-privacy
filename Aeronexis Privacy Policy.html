This policy explains what information Aeronexis Flight Simulator ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. The app does not require an account. If you use online or multiplayer features, a display name or callsign you choose may be shared with other players in the same session.

Information collected automatically.

  • Advertising data: our ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app interactions and approximate location to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase data: when you buy an item such as "Remove Ads", Google Play processes the payment. We receive confirmation that the purchase was made, not your payment card details.
  • Map data requests: when real-world map imagery and elevation are shown, the app requests map tiles from MapTiler. These requests include the map area being viewed and your IP address, which MapTiler receives as part of normal internet communication.
  • Diagnostics: Google Play may provide us with crash reports and performance data (Android vitals).

Information stored on your device. Settings, progress, unlocked aircraft and flight reports are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app and provide its features.
  • To show ads and let you unlock aircraft by watching rewarded ads.
  • To process and restore purchases.
  • To fix bugs and improve performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's privacy settings. You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Buying "Remove Ads" removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain available only if you choose to watch them to unlock content.

4. Third-party services

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • MapTiler: maptiler.com/privacy-policy

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users' safety.
